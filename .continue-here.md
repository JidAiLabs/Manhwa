`never drop a panel that owns a narration line`. Already documented with three
droppers in [[held-image-narrated-protection]]; this run found three more.
Nothing enforces it centrally, so each new drop site re-breaks it. Three codes
were three symptoms of the SAME rule, at three different stages:

- `552f438` **`system_card_unshown`** — `main()` called
  `drop_bubble_dominated_cuts` without `system_files`. `exempt_from_drop`
  withholds system-card protection from a panel that is not `recoverable`, and
  recoverability is judged on OCR/text evidence — so Ep54 p000111 (understood as
  "a blue system window containing text", `ocr_clean` **None**) read as a
  contentless husk and dropped. The function's own docstring already promised to
  exempt that set; the call site just never passed it.
- `9e17e6c` **`empty_item`** — `drop_caption_cards` returns an empty montage
  entry for a beat it believes is caption-only. Ep18 g0025: p000099 is
  `panel_kind=story` at 9% text coverage and its line describes a character "in
  wide-eyed horror", but `text_context_only_panel` read its subjects as
  text-only. Beat kept its line and 13.2s with NO cuts. `montage_files_for_beat`
  restores the beat's OWN art (never a neighbour's — that would reintroduce the
  p097x3 collapse) only when the line is real/non-filler.
- `0b45c43` **`long_hold`** — `substitute_garbage_sole_cuts` got
  `exempt=exempt_all` (seeded with `system_files`, never `narrated_files`) even
  though `protect_narrated_from_junk` gives narrated panels exactly that
  protection on the JUNK path. Swapped a narrated panel for a neighbour, which
  then held 16–25s. Proven cross-segment dups stay substitutable via the new

## ► TRAPS THAT PRODUCED FOUR WRONG NUMBERS THIS SESSION
Every one came from deriving a measure instead of importing the codebase's.
- `manifest.scenes.json` keys on **`panel_id`**, not `file`/`path`.
- `manifest.groups.json` holds **`shots`**, not `groups`.
- A split panel ships as `pN_a.jpg`+`pN_b.jpg`, so its base name never appears in
  `cuts[].file`. Raw matching said 46 losses / 54 eps; `prep_qa._base_scene` said
  21, and re-prepped chapters went to **zero** (Ep50 4→0, Ep8 3→0, Ep18 2→1, the
  1 a legitimate bubble fold). See [[shown-filename-is-identity]].
- `prep_qa` runs MULTIPLE times inside the auto-heal loop. A `panel_substituted`
  line in a job log may be from an intermediate pass — only the FINAL
  `prep_qa.json` is the end state. Across all 54 episodes the final reports carry
  **zero** `panel_substituted`, so line-to-art correctness is clean.
